## Mentor Program

In my junior year I joined DEDA’s STEM mentor program and was paired with two researchers from Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.

<Cols widths={[3, 2]}>
<Column>
<p class="indent">
    During my junior year in the Dublin Engineering Academy, I participated in the STEM Mentor Program, which paired me with 2 industry professionals.

    I had the honor of working with **Vincent Cheung**, a nuclear physcist at the Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, and **Nicholas Arnold-Medabalimi**, a Computational Physicst also at LNLL. As a scientist, Dr. Cheung contributes to a scientific database of nuclear interactions and taught me about the common misconceptions with quantam computing. Dr. Arnold-Medabalimi has an interesting job, bridging the gap between scientists and computer engineers, and I learned of a new role within the scientific research industry.

    My mentors helped me with various aspects of the my professional skills, such as how to approach networking events and organizing my resume. In addition, I gained insight about how to approach college research.

    Overall, I feel like I greatly improved my communication skills and resume writing skills. This program was important to my growth as an engineer because it focused on the people skills and presentation needed to get a job and fit comfortably in the workplace, making me a more well-rounded and employable individual.
</p>
