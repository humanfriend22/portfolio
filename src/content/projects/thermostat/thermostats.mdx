## 11/16

I printed a test plate to test the M3 screws I plan on using to attach the enclosure to the backplate. The plan is to assemble the relay board and the converter on the backplate with the ESP32 and temp sensor attached to the enclosure that wraps around the backplate. Then screws from the top and bottom into the sides of the backplate to secure it. I decided a support plate might be necessary since the LCD screen might get pressed hard so there are posts meant to support the PCB in common touch points.
