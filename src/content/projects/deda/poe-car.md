Between January 28 and February 24, 2025, my Principles of Engineering team built a car powered by up to two hydrogen fuel cells and solar cells using VEX parts, within an 8" x 12.5" footprint and a limited budget.

As **lead builder** I turned the team's design into a working prototype: the VEX chassis, a gear train driven by VEX EDR motors, and two solar panels and a hydrogen fuel cell wired together on a breadboard.

Early tests showed low power and mechanical instability. I led the troubleshooting and structural fixes, recorded in the team log, and came away with a firsthand sense of the real-world drawbacks of alternative energy sources.
