## [Abstract](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1SrqnUgjg7zeycQuroRvRuKWA_B598rT0V9qpCC3hRIs/edit?usp=sharing)

Improving our rover would involve many steps that take it out of the prototype phase. The first step would be making it more reliable and consistent at accomplishing the goals of the prototype. For example, by using larger rubber tires, the rover would not bottom out on the tiniest of hills. A lightweight profile would not require super powerful motors and make floatation easier. I noticed that our rover was very prone to getting stuck on vegetation and had almost no power.A wetlands rover will come across many foreign environments in the wild and should have an IP68 ingress protection rating.

## Material Selection
The VEX prototype mainly used steel and pool noodles to float in water, limiting its movement. Therefore, lightweight material such as carbon fiber or aluminium will allow the rover to float very well. An alternative to pool noodles is a ballast balloon or container. These are enclosed containers that can fill with water or stay filled with air making the rover to float. The tires can also have slight flaps that propel the rover in water. The enclosure would most likely be a box with rounded edges that sits pretty high to not get tangled in the vegetation.
