<p class="indent">
    The primary objective of the ArcGIS Volcano Shelters project was to achieve practical experience in the use of Geographic Information Systems (GIS) in real-world analysis with a particular focus on emergency preparedness in Hawaii. The objective was to learn how to operate the ArcGIS environment, edit map layers representing volcano hazard zones and emergency shelters, and use analysis tools to assess the availability of shelters to the populace. This involved exploring the map data, locating specific features like the Kilauea Rift Zone and Waiakeawaena Elementary School shelter by utilizing pop-up information, and performing the drive-time analysis to find out how much time it would take for residents of different locations to reach a shelter. Through this project, I have acquired an understanding of the fundamentals of using the ArcGIS Map Viewer interface and the potential of GIS in spatial data presentation and analysis for critical planning. Finding out how to add, query, and analyze different data layers (like lava flow hazard zones and shelters) was a worthwhile experience.
</p>
