<p class="indent">
    The goal of the Inch Cubed Project was to 3D print a design of our choosing using only 1in^3 worth of material.
</p>

For my project, I chose to model the Burj Khalifa. I started by importing a picture of the floor plan into Fusion 360 on which I traced a sketch of every floor. Then, I extruded each section to the height I needed and used a parameter to tweak the heights quickly. To save on material, I hollowed out the bottom layers as well as a cylinder about halfway up the model. Finally, once printed, the model came out to be 5.874”x1.539”x1.378”. While Fusion predicted the volume of the model to be 0.936 in^3, the 3D printing software estimated 1.565in^3.

During this project, I learned how to model a seemingly complex shape in Fusion 360 using features like sketches, canvases, extrusions and cuts. I also learned that the thinner a 3D printer prints at the top, the weaker the print will be. This is because a single layer is stronger connected to itself than to other layers. I also learned how to go from an idea to a finished project as there were many steps involved. This included brainstorming different ideas, sketching a few to see what I wanted to do, sketching a detailed plan of what I wanted to model, modeling my project and revising the model to fit within the constraints of the 3D printer and the project guidelines.
