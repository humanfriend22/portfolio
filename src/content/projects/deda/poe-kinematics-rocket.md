The objective of this project is to design, build, and test a functional rocket using only the provided materials (paper and tape). We must work in teams to brainstorm, sketch, and refine multiple design ideas before selecting two final prototypes for construction. The rockets will be launched on a football field, and the maximum distance traveled will be recorded and analyzed. Each team must document their design process, create detailed annotated sketches, and ensure their rockets meet all given constraints. The challenge is to optimize the design to achieve the greatest possible flight distance while adhering to material limitations.

I learned how different types of fins affected the aerodynamics of a rocket and the importance of a well designed cone. I was the lead builder on this project, designing the fins and cone. One challenge I overcame was figuring how to get the right shape for the cone and I sovled this by improvising with packing tape in a diagonal fashion on top of my existing cone to make a very sharp point. Then I added a layer of packing tape around the seal to make it smooth.
