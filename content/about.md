# About

I’m Saketh. I build things, mostly where software meets hardware, and I want them to be useful. I never saw the point of making another to-do app just for practice. I’d rather spend the time on something my family or my team will actually use.

## How I got here

My mom says it started with outlets and switches I could barely reach when I was two. Then came freestyle Lego, Scratch in third grade, and assembling every piece of IKEA furniture we bought. In middle school I taught myself web development, and I kept going into full-stack and backend work.

For a long time, though, there was a gap between what I wanted to build and what I knew how to build. Closing it is most of what the projects on this site are about. Software on its own never felt like enough, since I don’t want to spend my life making screens light up. I want to build the whole thing: the board, the firmware, and the code around it.

## What I’m like

- **Useful over impressive.** My thermostats and garage opener exist because walking to them annoyed me. The website I built for my robotics club exists because the old one didn’t do it justice.
- **I like knowing why it works.** Freya Holmér’s animated explanations of Bézier curves and splines have stuck with me for years, and they’re the reason I enjoy math that I can watch happen.
- **Thoughtful, sometimes too much.** I spend a lot of time thinking about how I think. It can feel like overthinking, but it’s also how I debug myself.

## Beyond the screen

- I’ve lived in the Bay Area my whole life. I grew up speaking English and Telugu, my mother tongue, and I’m Indian-American.
- In my spare time I sleep and play video games.
- I’d love to travel. Beautiful views are easy to find online, so I’m more curious about the journey and the people you meet on the way there.
