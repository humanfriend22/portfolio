## Overview

Battle of the Bay is a VEX Robotics Competition event hosted by Gael Force Robotics. I built the intermission website shown on the live stream, and the repo includes the OBS configuration we used, along with TM Switcher and the NDI plugin.

At the event I was assigned to run the skills field, and before and during setup day I helped debug live-stream issues.
