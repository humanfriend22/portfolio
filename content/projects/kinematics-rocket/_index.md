## Overview

From March 25 to April 24, 2025, my team designed, built, and tested rockets made only of paper and tape. We brainstormed, chose two prototypes, and launched them on the football field to record the maximum distance.

As **lead builder** I designed the fins and cone. To get a sharp tip, I layered packing tape diagonally over the cone, then added a smoothing layer around the seal. It taught me how fin design affects aerodynamics and why a well-shaped cone matters.
