## Overview

I joined Gael Force Robotics at the end of freshman year. In my sophomore year I was the solo, and therefore lead, programmer, and I now captain the team. My job is the autonomous period: code that drives the robot on its own for the first part of every match.

## What I do

- **PID control and odometry** to move precisely around the field. I researched these algorithms over the summer, including tuning the constants.
- **Templates:** our code builds on the open-source [RW-Template](https://github.com/richardbwang/RW-Template) by Richard Wang, which provides PID, odometry, and boomerang-style motion. My repos for the Push Back season ([`pushback`](https://github.com/gael-force-resurgence/pushback)) and later ([`dakota`](https://github.com/gael-force-resurgence/dakota)) adapt it to our robots.
- **Debugging hardware:** my routines qualified the team for state championships despite poorly built odometry trackers, drivetrain instability, and a recurring white-screen bug on the competition brain.

## Results

- Qualified for **state** in one of the most competitive VEX regions in the world.
- Traveled to a **signature event in Wisconsin**, and as captain this year went to about ten competitions with a team of about ten, including a signature event in North Dakota.
- Mentor to the captains who now run my old team.
