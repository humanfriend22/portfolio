## Mentor program

In my junior year I joined DEDA’s STEM mentor program and was paired with two researchers from Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.

{{% cards %}}
{{% info-card type="Mentor" title="Vincent Cheung" %}}
Nuclear physicist. He contributes to a scientific database of nuclear interactions and talked through common misconceptions about quantum computing.
{{% /info-card %}}
{{% info-card type="Mentor" title="Nicholas Arnold-Medabalimi" %}}
Computational physicist. His role bridges scientists and computer engineers, which introduced me to a kind of research job I didn’t know existed.
{{% /info-card %}}
{{% /cards %}}

They helped me with networking event strategies, organizing my resume, and approaching college research. The biggest takeaway was how much communication and presentation matter for fitting into a workplace.
